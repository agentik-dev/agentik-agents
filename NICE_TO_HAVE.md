- **S4 · bin/claude-local: a signal inside the router start's `$( … )` orphans the router** (F2, the a-s1 adversary, 2026-10-13). A signal that lands after `STARTED="$( nohup "$ROUTER_BIN" --serve … & echo "$!" )"` has forked the router but before the assignment completes leaves `STARTED` empty, so the EXIT trap stops nothing and the router is left with ppid 1. Repro: run a copy of `claude-local` beside a stub router (as `tests/test-localrig.sh` does) on a cold port and send the launcher SIGTERM about 1 ms after it prints `starting agentroute`; 200 runs left 2 routers running. The window is about 1 ms.
- **S4 · bin/claude-local: until its next poll, a launcher can still stop a router another launcher is using** (the a-s1 builder, 2026-10-13; not reproduced). F1's fix clears `STARTED` at the first poll that finds the router answering. For up to one poll interval (0.25 s) after the router starts answering, launcher B may already have found it and exec'd claude while launcher A has not yet polled. If A dies from a signal in that interval, its EXIT trap stops B's router. Before F1's fix, this window covered A's whole preflight.
